# Ping

Your ping to the game server, in a small widget you can drag anywhere on screen and lock in place.

- The pill turns green, amber or red by how good the ping is; you choose where each colour ends.
- Lock it and clicks pass through to the game. Double-click it to open the settings.
- Three sizes, with or without its background and signal bars.
- Shown only while AION2 is open.
- The game's FPS beside the ping, if you turn it on (meter 2.0.78 or newer, Windows). Counting frames needs the meter to run as administrator: turning it on asks you to restart the meter so, and it stays so until you turn **Run as administrator** off in the meter's Settings.

Needs A2Tools DPS Meter 2.0.73 or newer (the meter's `ping` event).
