# Death Recap

After a player kills you, who they were and what they did to you.

- **Last death:** their name, legion, server, class, level, combat power and gear score, the damage they did to you, in how many hits, the fight's length and their DPS, and the skills they used.
- **History:** every death kept; click one to see it again.
- **Tally:** deaths counted by player, legion or server.
- **Popup:** a corner toast when you die (or a centre banner, a slim strip, or none). Drag it to move it; click it to open the window.

Only after a death: the meter tells this plugin nothing about other players while you live, so it can't warn of anyone nearby.

Needs A2Tools DPS Meter 2.0.81 or newer (the meter's `deathRecap` event).
